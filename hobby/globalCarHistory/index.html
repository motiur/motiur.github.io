1886

Benz Patent-Motorwagen

Carl Benz · Mannheim

Patented in 1886, Carl Benz’s three-wheeled Motorwagen is widely regarded as the first practical petrol-powered automobile. Bertha Benz’s pioneering long-distance journey in 1888 helped demonstrate that the new machine could be used beyond the workshop.
