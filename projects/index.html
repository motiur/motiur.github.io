Current research

Bengali Visual Speech Recognition (Lip Reading)2024 – Present

Co-Investigator: Dr. Mahady Hasan — Independent University, Bangladesh

  • Multimodal lip-reading pipeline targeting Bangladesh’s ~13.7 million deaf community.
  • Graph Attention Network (GAT) + Transformer over MediaPipe-extracted lip landmarks (40 landmarks from 478 facial keypoints); BiLSTM / attention-based temporal modelling.
  • Trained on the LRW dataset with A100 GPU (Google Colab); optimised Drive I/O via in-RAM dataset loading.
  • Funded by the IUB VC Research Fund and IUB Sponsored Research (2-year duration); aligned with UN SDG 10 & SDG 4.
